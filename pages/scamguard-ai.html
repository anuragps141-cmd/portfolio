📚 Scam Prevention Education Resources

🎣 Phishing Attacks

  • Check sender email addresses carefully
  • Look for generic greetings like "Dear Customer"
  • Verify links before clicking (hover to see actual URL)
  • Be suspicious of urgent requests for sensitive info
  • Enable two-factor authentication on accounts

💰 Investment Scams

  • Promises of guaranteed returns are red flags
  • Verify investment opportunities through official channels
  • Check if advisors are properly registered
  • Be wary of pressure to invest quickly
  • Research companies independently

💔 Romance Scams

  • Be skeptical of professions requiring overseas work
  • Avoid sending money to people you haven't met
  • Use reverse image search on photos
  • Video call before meeting in person
  • Trust your gut if something feels off

🔧 Tech Support Scams

  • Legitimate tech companies don't call unexpectedly
  • Never give remote access to strangers
  • Use official company support channels
  • Be suspicious of urgent pop-ups
  • Verify claims independently

🎰 Lottery Scams

  • You can't win what you didn't enter
  • Legitimate lotteries don't ask for upfront fees
  • Watch for poor grammar and spelling
  • Never provide banking info to claim prizes
  • Verify through official lottery websites

🛡️ General Protection Tips

  • Keep passwords strong and unique
  • Update software regularly
  • Use reputable antivirus software
  • Be cautious on public WiFi
  • Monitor your credit reports
